Ringkasan Role & Fitur

Role Aksi dalam Flowchart Hasil/Output
KARYAWAN Buat permintaan (destination, purpose, datetime) Request berstatus PENDING
MANAGER Review & Approve/Tolak request Status: MANAGER_APPROVED/REJECTED
ADMIN Assign vehicle + driver atau Tolak Status: ADMIN_APPROVED + vehicle assignment
DRIVER Mulai/selesai perjalanan, catat KM UsageRecord tersimpan, status COMPLETED

Catatan Penting:

  • Notifikasi otomatis dikirim ke step berikutnya dalam flow
  • Vehicle availability berubah otomatis (available/in_use)
  • UsageRecord menyimpan data KM untuk laporan
